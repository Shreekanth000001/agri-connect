# 🌾 Agri-Connect

> A digital mandi platform that connects farmers directly with buyers — eliminating middlemen through a transparent bidding system.

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-Demo-success?style=flat-square&logo=vercel)](https://agri-connect-it.vercel.app)
[![Next.js](https://img.shields.io/badge/Next.js-000000?style=flat-square&logo=nextdotjs)](https://nextjs.org)
[![Prisma](https://img.shields.io/badge/Prisma-2D3748?style=flat-square&logo=prisma)](https://prisma.io)

---

## 🚩 Problem It Solves

Indian farmers lose 30-40% of their earnings to middlemen in traditional mandis. Agri-Connect gives farmers a direct channel to list their produce and lets buyers place competitive bids — ensuring fair pricing for both sides.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🧑‍🌾 **Farmer dashboard** — list produce with quantity, price, and harvest details
- ⚖️ **Bidding system** — transparent auction mechanism for fair pricing
- 🔐 **Authentication** — secure role-based access for farmers and buyers
- 📍 **Interactive proximity map** — renders farmer and buyer locations on a Leaflet.js map 
  with a straight-line displacement measurement, helping buyers gauge distance before bidding
